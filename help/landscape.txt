<p spacebelow=10>When you rotate a mobile device, the app's display will rotate with it.  But you can, if you wish, lock the app's screen so that it will not rotate when you rotate the device.</p>
<p spacebelow=10>In order to lock or unlock the screen rotation, press and hold the gear icon in the upper right of the screen.  Normally when you press this icon, it brings up the Settings dialog box.  But if you press and hold the icon for more than a second or so, it will display a lock icon showing the status of the rotation lock.  An unlocked image means the display is unlocked and will rotate as you rotate your device.  A red locked image means the display is locked and will not rotate when you rotate your device.</p>
<p spacebelow=10><img src="binfile:help/images/rotation-icons.png"></p>
<p spacebelow=10>By default, the display rotation is unlocked.  So if you press and hold the Settings icon, you will see the unlocked icon.</p>
<p spacebelow=10><img src="binfile:help/images/rotation-unlocked.png"></p>
<p spacebelow=10>If you click on the image, it will briefly change to a red locked image and the text will say "Locked!" before the image disappears.  If you do not wish to lock the display, simply press anywhere on the screen other than the lock image and the display will return to normal without changing the lock status.</p>
<p spacebelow=10>Likewise, if the display is locked and you wish to unlock it, press and hold the gear icon for a couple of seconds, and you will see the red locked image.</p>
<p spacebelow=10><img src="binfile:help/images/rotation-locked.png"></p>
<p spacebelow=10>Click on the image to unlock the display.  The image will briefly change to the image which says "Unlocked!" before disappearing.  Like before, if you do not wish to change anything, click anywhere other than the red image.</p>
<p spacebelow=10>When the rotation is locked, the gear icon in the upper right which you long press to turn the rotation lock on and off will have a dot in its center.</p>
<p spacebelow=10><img src="binfile:help/images/rotation_gear_dot.png"></p>
<p spacebelow=10>The lock status of the display is saved between sessions.  So when you reopen the app, the display will still be locked or unlocked as you last set it.</p>
<p spacebelow=10>Please note that although all screens can be displayed in both landscape and portrait mode, not all screens can accommodate landscape mode.  Some screens on smaller devices do not work well in landscape mode.  Here's the Search screen in landscape mode on a small screen.</p>
<p spacebelow=10><img src="binfile:help/images/landscape-search.png"></p>
<p spacebelow=10>As you can see, with the mobile device's onscreen keyboard displayed so you can type in the search field, you cannot see any of your matches.</p>
<p spacebelow=10>Or here is the verb study field in Portrait mode where you can easily see the entire description of the verb.</p>
<p spacebelow=10><img src="binfile:help/images/portrait-verbstudy.png"></p>
<p spacebelow=10>But if we turn our phone to landscape mode, there is very little room and you must scroll to see all the description.</p>
<p spacebelow=10><img src="binfile:help/images/landscape-verbstudy.png"></p>
<p spacebelow=10>On smaller devices, you will find that most screens in the app work better in portrait mode.</p>
<p spacebelow=10>In addition, some dialog boxes will not fit in landscape mode.  If you rotate your device and there is not enough room to display the entire dialog box, you will need to scroll the dialog box up and down to see all the options.  You can scroll anywhere on the screen; you do not need to be touching the dialog box itself.  In fact, it's often easier to swipe up and down to one side of the actual dialog box so that you do not accidently trigger any buttons.</p>
<p spacebelow=10>Here's the Settings dialog box from the Read screen displayed in landscape mode.  As you can see, the bottom of the box is cut off and you cannot see (or access) the "Accept" or "Cancel" buttons to close the dialog box.</p>
<p spacebelow=10><img src="binfile:help/images/settings-landscape.png"></p>
<p spacebelow=10>To access the parts of the dialog box which are below the screen, you can scroll the dialog box down.  Now the top will be above the level which you can see, but you can access the bottom of the dialog box.</p>
<p spacebelow=10><img src="binfile:help/images/settings-landscape-scrolled.png"></p>
<p spacebelow=10><i>You could also rotate your device back to portrait mode.  Then you will be able to see and access the entire dialog box.</i></p>
<p spacebelow=10>In other cases, dialog boxes may fit on the screen, but when the mobile keyboard is activated it "pushes" the dialog box up so that it's no longer usable.  Here's a Save dialog box which has been pushed up by the mobile keyboard so that you can no longer see the field in which to type.</p>
<p spacebelow=10><img src="binfile:help/images/landscape-pushup.png"></p>
<p spacebelow=10>In this case, simply rotate the device back to portrait mode.  Then you can open the dialog box again and you should be able to access all of it.</p>
<p spacebelow=10>In short, if you find options which are disabled or become unusable in landscape mode, rotate your device to portrait mode, and they should be usable again.</p>
