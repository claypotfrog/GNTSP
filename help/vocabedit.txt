<h3>Renaming a Vocabulary List and Group</h3>
<p spacebelow=10>On either the Vocabulary List screen or the Vocabulary Groups screen, you can rename any of the lists or groups.  First click on "Edit" in the upper right corner to put the lists into EDIT mode.</p>
<p spacebelow=10><img src="binfile:help/images/vocab-parentlist-edit.png"></p>
<p spacebelow=10>While in EDIT mode, click on any title to rename it.</p>
<p spacebelow=10><img src="binfile:help/images/vocab-parentlist-rename.png"></p>
<p spacebelow=10>You can also click and drag any line's reorder bars (the three gray horizontal lines on the right side) to reorder the list on this screen.  In the image below, we have dragged the first line of the list, "All Words by Frequency", to make it the second line of the list.  (<i>On Android you cannot click and drag the reorder bars directly.  First click on the bars of the row you wish to reorder.  This will put that row into "reorder" mode.  Then you can click anywhere on that row and drag it to a new position.</i>)</p>
<p spacebelow=10><img src="binfile:help/images/vocab-parentlist-reorder.png"></p>
<p spacebelow=10>While in EDIT mode, clicking on the red half circle on the left side will bring up more options for that list.  In the image below we have clicked on the icon on the second line, "All Verbs by Frequency", which brings up the "Share", "Export", "Reset", and "Delete" buttons for that list.</p>
<p spacebelow=10><img src="binfile:help/images/vocab-parentlist-showoptions.png"></p>

<p spacebelow=10>See "Sharing Vocabulary" on the "About" page entitled  for information how to share vocabulary lists with others using the program.</p>

<h3>Resetting a Vocabulary List or Group</h3>
<p spacebelow=10>Clicking on the blue "Reset" button will reset all the words in that vocabulary list or group so that you will review them again immediately the next time you review any words from this list.  If you do this for a vocabulary group, then only that group is reset.  If you do this for a vocabulary list, then all the groups in the list are reset.</p>
<p spacebelow=10><img src="binfile:help/images/vocab-reset.png"></p>
<h3>Deleting a List or Group</h3>
<p spacebelow=10>Clicking on the red "Delete" button will delete this vocabulary list or group.  Once deleted, a list cannot be recovered.  (<i>If you have shared a vocabulary list or group, however, then you can re-import it.  See the section on "Sharing Vocabulary" on the "About" page for information how to share vocabulary lists.</i>)</p>
<p spacebelow=10>Click anywhere else in order to return to the normal EDIT mode screen.</p>
<h3>Editing Vocabulary Groups</h3>
<p spacebelow=10>After you have created a vocabulary group (see the section on "Creating Vocabulary" on the "About" page), you can examine all the words in the group as well as add new words or delete existing words.</p>
<p spacebelow=10>You can examine the words in the group by clicking on the button for that group which says "Examine X Words" where "X" tells you the number of words the list contains.  Here we are looking at the groups we created in the "Creating Vocabulary" section which includes all the words in the gospel of Mark which appear less than 10 times in the entire Greek New Testament.</p>
<p spacebelow=10><img src="binfile:help/images/vocab-childlist-mark-groups.png"></p>
<p spacebelow=10>If we click on the link which says "Examine 41 Words" from the first group, "Mark Chp. 1", it takes us to a page where we see all the words which are part of this group.  (These words are listed in Alphabetical order the first time you enter this page.  But you can click on the blue line which says "Greek Word ascending" next to the label "Sort Results by" to see a drop-down menu to change the order of the lines.  Whatever you choose will remain the order every time you examine the words of a vocabulary group until you change it using the menu or close the program.)</p>
<p spacebelow=10><img src="binfile:help/images/vocab-childlist-viewwords.png"></p>
<p spacebelow=10>Here you see all the words in this vocabulary group along with their definitions, how many times that word appears in the Greek New Testament, and when we will next see this word when we are reviewing this group.</p>
<h3>Editing a Word</h3>
<p spacebelow=10>If you click directly on a word in this view, it will take you to the Details page for that word (or the root form of that word if it is not a dictionary word).  But you can edit the entry by putting the list into EDIT mode.  Click the button in the upper right which says "Edit" and the list goes into EDIT mode - now there are red half-circles next to every word.</p>
<p spacebelow=10><img src="binfile:help/images/vocab-childlist-viewwords-edit.png"></p>
<p spacebelow=10>Now when you click on a word, the Greek word and the gloss will be copied into a dialog box where you can edit them.  In the image below, we have clicked on the first entry, "ἄγριος, α, ον", and that text as well as the gloss, "wild, fierce" has been put into the Edit dialog box.</p>
<p spacebelow=10><img src="binfile:help/images/vocab-childlist-viewwords-editdialog.png"></p>
<p spacebelow=10>You can edit both the Greek text and the gloss in this dialog box.  (See "Typing in Greek" for information how to use the built-in Greek keyboard.)  Let's say we want to expand the gloss a bit.  We edit the gloss to be "wild, savage, harsh"</p>
<p spacebelow=10><img src="binfile:help/images/vocab-childlist-viewwords-editdialog2.png"></p>
<p spacebelow=10>Now click the "Accept" button to save these changes.  (Click "Cancel", obviously, to cancel this dialog box and leave the entry as it was.)  If we have changed a dictionary word, then the program will warn you that you are changing an entry from the dictionary entry to a custom entry.</p>
<p spacebelow=10><img src="binfile:help/images/vocab-childlist-viewwords-editdialog-warning.png"></p>
<p spacebelow=10>As the warning says, you are only changing the gloss for THIS vocabulary list.  Everywhere else in the program, ἄγριος will still have the gloss "wild, fierce".  If you wish to change the gloss everywhere, then click on the word when the list is NOT in EDIT mode.  This takes you to Details page for this word.  If you change its gloss on the Details page, it is changed everywhere in the app.</p>
<p spacebelow=10>But assuming this is what you want, click "Continue" and you'll see that the gloss for this entry has changed to edited version.</p>
<p spacebelow=10><img src="binfile:help/images/vocab-childlist-viewwords-edited.png"></p>

Unless you have Vocabulary delete warnings turned off (see the section on "Vocabulary Warnings" on the "About" page), the program will ask you to confirm the deletion.</p>

<h3>Adding new words</h3>
<p spacebelow=10>There are multiple ways to add new words to an existing vocabulary group.  We can add them manually, or we can add them from a word's details page, or we can add them from the search page.</p>
<h3>Adding words manually</h3>
<p spacebelow=10>To manually add more words to this vocabulary group, click on the "Edit" button in the upper right to put the list into EDIT mode, and then click the blue "+" ("plus") that appears on the upper left.  This take us to the Vocabulary Creator page.  This is the same page that we used to create Vocabulary groups in the first place.  But this time we are placed on the "Manual Add" screen.</p>
<p spacebelow=10><img src="binfile:help/images/vocab-details-add.png"></p>
<p spacebelow=10>See the section "Adding Vocabulary Manually" in "Creating Vocabulary" on the "About" page for instruction how to add words manually to a vocabulary group using this screen.
<h3>Adding words from the Details and Search screens</h3>
<p spacebelow=10>In order to add words to a vocabulary group from the Details or Search screen, you first need to "star" that vocabulary group.  On the right side of every vocabulary group in the vocabulary groups screen, there is the blue outline of a star.  If you click on that icon, it will turn solid blue, and that vocabulary group is now "starred".  This means that you can add words directly to that group.  Here is our trusty "Mark" Vocabulary list that we created in the "Creating Vocabulary" section and we have been working with ever since.</p>
<p spacebelow=10><img src="binfile:help/images/vocab-childlist-mark-all-chp.png"></p>
<p spacebelow=10>Let's create a new empty group here that we will use to add new words.  Create an empty group as described in the "Creating Vocabulary" section of the "About" page, and then rename it "Cool Words in Mark".  So here's our Mark vocabulary list with the new group.</p>
<p spacebelow=10><img src="binfile:help/images/vocab-childlist-coolmark.png"></p>
<p spacebelow=10>Now click this blue star outline for this group, and it will turn solid.  We have "starred" this group so we can add words directly to it.</p>
<p spacebelow=10><img src="binfile:help/images/vocab-childlist-coolmark-starred.png"></p>
<p spacebelow=10>Now imagine as we are reading, we come across this word in Mark 1:43, "ἐμβριμάομαι", "to snort".</p>
<p spacebelow=10><img src="binfile:help/images/vocab-snort.png"></p>
<p spacebelow=10>We <i>definitely</i> want to remember how to say "snort" in Koine Greek!  So we click the blue box to go the details page for this word.</p>
<p spacebelow=10><img src="binfile:help/images/vocab-snort-details.png"></p>
<p spacebelow=10>The last line of the details says "Add word to vocabulary".  If you click that line, you will see a drop-down menu of vocabulary lists you can add this word to.  The first line always says "Vocabulary Creator Field".  This is the manual entry field on the Vocabulary Creator page.  You can always add a word there and deal with it later.  But any vocabulary groups which you have "starred" will also appear in this menu.  Since we starred the group "Cool Words in Mark" group in the "Mark" vocabulary list, that group shows up in the menu.</p>
<p spacebelow=10><img src="binfile:help/images/vocab-details-add-menu.png"></p>
<p spacebelow=10>If we click that line, "Cool Words in Mark", then this word will automatically added to that group.  Now if we go back and look at our "Cool Words in Mark" vocabulary group, we see that it is no longer empty, but has one word in it - "ἐμβριμάομαι".</p>
<p spacebelow=10><img src="binfile:help/images/vocab-details-snort-added.png"></p>
<p spacebelow=10>We can also add words to groups from the Search page in the same manner.  When we choose to add a word (or words) from the search page to a vocabulary group, we will see this same drop-down menu.  Whichever group name we click on in that menu, the word(s) will be added to that group.  Please see "Searching" on the "About" page for more information and images of how to do this.</p>
